# CLAUDE.md: The Hapa Edit

Content rules for any Claude session working on hapaedit.com. The working desk (backlog, plan, scratch notes) is the private Claude Doc "The Hapa Edit: Journal Desk".

## Public content
- The site is public. Never mention employer, personal finance, or benefits details in anything public.
- Voice: first person, open on the point, one test the reader can do today, real prices and dates, expensive by restraint. No hype words (harness, unlock, seamless, revolution, synergy).
- Never invent memories, family details or prices. Leave `[Alison: …]` slots instead.
- Real photos of Alison only. Product images come only from the brand or its affiliate program, never scraped.
- Skincare posts carry patch-test and SPF lines and make no medical claims.

## Site structure
- Nav: Hapa (wordmark), The Edit (blog), Quizzes, The Store, About.
- The Edit: `/the-edit/{slug}/`. Quizzes are a library with descriptive titles at `/quizzes/{slug}/`; results are emailed to the visitor.
- Japanese locale (`/ja/`) is parked for a future rollout.

## SEO / AEO
- Every title targets a real search query with checked volume (Semrush first, Ubersuggest second).
- Answer the query in the first two sentences. Use question-style H2s, plus FAQ and author markup.

## The Store
- Feature anything Alison bought.
- Link via the brand's own affiliate program first. If there isn't one, use the lowest-priced retailer with US sizes 7, 8 and 9 in stock that runs a program.
- Every tracked link is labeled "Affiliate", and each page carries one disclosure line.
- Alison submits every affiliate application and accepts the terms herself.

## Idea intake
- Messages starting "Hapa idea:" get an HE-### ID in the backlog plus scratch notes: dek, hook, 3–5 beats, one reader test, shop tie-in, `[Alison:]` slots.
- Pace: one finished piece a week.
